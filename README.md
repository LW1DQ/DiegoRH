# Diego R. Rodriguez Herlein

### Researcher | Lecturer | PhD Candidate in Computer Science

Universidad Nacional de la Patagonia Austral (UNPA), Argentina

## About Me

My research focuses on computer networks, Internet of Things (IoT), machine learning, network simulation, and time-series analysis.

I am particularly interested in reproducible computational research and the application of predictive models to network performance analysis.

## Research Interests

- IoT and Wi-Fi networks
- Routing protocols: AODV, OLSR, and DSDV
- Network simulation with ns-3
- Machine learning and deep learning
- Network traffic prediction and time-series analysis
- Statistical modeling and reproducible experiments

## Current Research

- Performance analysis and optimization of routing protocols in Wi-Fi-based IoT networks.
- Predictive modeling of real network traffic using statistical and deep learning approaches.
- Research workflows supporting reproducibility and scientific rigor.

Scientific rigor, reproducibility, transparency, and evidence-based conclusions.
